## Notation used by every clause of this part

- **binary64**: the IEEE-754 double format: sign, 11-bit exponent, 52-bit fraction. A positive finite value is
  m × 2^e with integer m < 2^53 and e ≥ −1074; it is *normal* when m ≥ 2^52 and *subnormal* when e = −1074 and
  m < 2^52. The largest finite value is (2^53 − 1) × 2^971 = `1.7976931348623157e308`; the smallest positive
  value is 2^−1074 = `5e-324`.
- **RNE** (round to nearest, ties to even): the representable binary64 value nearest to an exact real x; when x
  is exactly halfway between two neighbours, the neighbour whose m is even. Representable values below 2^−1022
  are the multiples of 2^−1074, so a result in that range is rounded ONCE, directly to that grid (gradual
  underflow); an exact x with \|x\| ≤ 2^−1075 gives zero (the halfway point 2^−1075 goes to zero because zero is
  the even neighbour). An exact x with \|x\| ≥ 2^1024 − 2^970 gives infinity.
- One **IEEE operation** means: take the two binary64 operands as exact rationals, form the exact product or
  quotient, apply RNE once. Nothing else (no extended precision, no fused operations) happens in the original.
- **S**: the unsigned 64-bit decimal significand collected from a JSON literal. **E10**: the signed decimal
  exponent adjustment collected while reading digits. **X**: the magnitude written after `e`/`E`.
  **T**: the total decimal exponent. **F0** = RNE(S). **P(k)**: the power-of-ten table entry (S4.112).
